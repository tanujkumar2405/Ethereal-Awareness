About the initiative

Professional inquiry, spiritual clarity and sensitive research.

Ethereal Awareness is managed by Tanuj Kumar, Issac Joseph, and Ayush Sinha, empowering modern seekers with structured training, evidence-based study, and compassionate psychic support.

Our Story

Founded to support rigorous paranormal education without sensationalism.

Ethereal Awareness began as a response to the need for credible, accessible training in paranormal investigation, energy sensitivity and consciousness studies. We bridge traditional wisdom with contemporary scientific practice.

Managed by the Core Team

Tanuj Kumar is a paranormal investigator, researcher, and equipment specialist who ensures every student learns reliable field practice and evidence-based documentation.

  • Expert field support with investigative tools
  • Practical training for research-led paranormal work
  • Structured methodology for safe, credible outcomes

Guided by experienced practitioners

Issac Joseph is a psychic, tarot card reader, and intuition trainer who leads psychic sessions, tarot consultations, and guided energy awareness workshops. Ayush Sinha supports program delivery with practical insight and training coordination.

Our Mission

To cultivate a scholarly community of researchers, psychics, and spiritual mentors who approach subtle work with discipline, care, and accountability.

We create pathways that empower students to practice ethically, support clear communication, and achieve credible outcomes in both private sessions and field research.

Our Vision

To normalize consciousness inquiry as a respected domain of study and service, where intuition, energy awareness and investigative integrity co-exist with professionalism.

Ethics & Research

Solid principles for every course and consultation.

Independent Verification

Field data and anecdotal experience are documented with clear evidence logs, sensory corroboration, and independent review whenever possible.

Client Respect

Confidentiality, informed consent and culturally sensitive communication are embedded into every session and training module.

Practical Safety

Environmental awareness and self-regulation are taught alongside psychic practices to promote grounded, sustainable work.

Methodology

Structured learning that supports research, spiritual growth and professional readiness.

Our methodology blends observation, instrumentation, energy calibration, guided exercises and peer review. Every student learns how to apply understanding in the field and in private consultation.

  • Clear research objectives and outcome tracking.
  • Hands-on practice with ethical assessment.
  • Integration of psychic skill with conscious clarity.
